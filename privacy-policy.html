Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. Introduction

Hustle Ledger is built with a privacy-first approach. We are committed to protecting your data and ensuring complete transparency about how your information is handled.

By using the app, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Hustle Ledger is designed to collect as little data as possible.

  • Financial Data: Income and expense entries you manually add.
  • App Preferences: Settings such as tax rate and usage preferences.

This data is stored only on your device.

We do NOT collect or access:

  • Personal identity information (name, email, address)
  • Bank or payment details
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage analytics or tracking data

3. How Your Data Is Used

All data remains on your device and is used only to provide core app functionality such as calculations, reports, and insights.

We do not transmit, share, or sell your data.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is securely stored locally on your device. Hustle Ledger does not use external servers or cloud storage.

You are fully in control of your data.

5. Third-Party Services

Hustle Ledger does not use third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Since all data is stored locally, you can delete all your data at any time by removing the app from your device.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy in the future. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
