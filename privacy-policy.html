Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 1, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Hustle Ledger ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

By using Hustle Ledger, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Hustle Ledger is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal information:

  • Financial Data: Income and expense entries you manually input into the app. This data is stored locally on your device only.
  • App Settings: Your preferences such as tax rate selection and premium status, stored locally on your device.

We do NOT collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, address, SSN)
  • Bank account or credit card information
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers for tracking
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data

3. How We Use Your Information

All data you enter into Hustle Ledger is:

  • Stored exclusively on your device using local storage
  • Never transmitted to our servers
  • Never shared with third parties
  • Used solely to provide the app's features (income tracking, tax estimation, reports)

4. Data Storage & Security

Your financial data is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. We do not operate any servers that store your personal financial information. Your data remains on your device and is only accessible through the app.

If you delete the app, all locally stored data will be permanently removed from your device.

5. In-App Purchases

Hustle Ledger offers a one-time Premium upgrade ($9.99) processed through Google Play. We do not collect or store your payment information. All payment processing is handled securely by Google Play's billing system.

6. Third-Party Services

Hustle Ledger uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Billing: For processing in-app purchases. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Expo (React Native): Our development framework. No personal data is collected through this framework.

7. Children's Privacy

Hustle Ledger is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

9. Disclaimer

Hustle Ledger provides income and expense tracking tools and tax estimates for organizational purposes only. The app does not provide tax advice. Tax estimates are based on user-selected rates and may not reflect your actual tax liability. Please consult a qualified tax professional for personalized tax guidance.
